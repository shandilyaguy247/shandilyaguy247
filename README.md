## 👨‍💻 About Me

I'm **Harshit Shandilya**, a Software Engineer specializing in **Site Reliability Engineering (SRE)** at **Google DeepMind** in Mountain View, California, where I focus on large-scale network capacity, traffic placement, and ML/AI serving infrastructure.

Previously, I worked as a Senior Software Engineer at **RELX** and as a Software Engineer at **Cisco** in San Jose, CA. I hold a Master of Science (M.S.) in Electrical and Computer Engineering from **Purdue University**, completed robotics and additive manufacturing research at the **Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)** in Germany, and earned my B.Tech in Electronics and Communication Engineering from **Vellore Institute of Technology (VIT)**.

## 🔍 Technical Interests & Expertise

I specialize in building resilient, high-throughput distributed systems and infrastructure:

- **Site Reliability & Distributed Systems:** Large-scale production reliability, fault tolerance, system design, and automated incident mitigation.
- **Cloud & Network Infrastructure:** Network capacity planning, traffic engineering, edge computing, and cloud networking.
- **ML/AI Infrastructure:** High-availability serving infrastructure and scalable backend systems for machine learning workloads.
- **Programming Languages:** Proficient in **Python**, **C/C++**, **Go**, and **Java**.
- **Software Failure Analysis:** Empirical analysis of software failure patterns to engineer higher-reliability systems.

## 🌱 Current Focus

My current engineering endeavors involve:

- **AI & ML Serving Reliability:** Scaling infrastructure and capacity for next-generation AI workloads at Google.
- **Traffic Placement & Network Optimization:** Engineering deterministic, fault-tolerant network routing and capacity architectures.
- **Backend & Systems Architecture:** Designing low-latency, high-availability distributed backend services.
- **Empirical Software Engineering:** Applying failure knowledge and observability insights to large-scale system design.
